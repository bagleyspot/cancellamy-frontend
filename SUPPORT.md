# Getting help

- **Documentation** (Italian): in the [backend repository](https://github.com/bagleyspot/cancellamy-backend/blob/main/docs/README.md). Frontend design notes: [docs/FRONTEND-NOTES.md](./docs/FRONTEND-NOTES.md).
- **Questions and ideas**: [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/bagleyspot/cancellamy-frontend/discussions).
- **Bugs**: use the [issue forms](https://github.com/bagleyspot/cancellamy-frontend/issues/new/choose). Registry corrections go to the [backend repository](https://github.com/bagleyspot/cancellamy-backend/issues/new/choose).
- **Security problems**: never in public, see [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

Cancellamy is maintained by volunteers and does not give legal advice.
